# Invoice Generator

## Business Tooling & Automation

Invoice Generator is a software project focused on a common business workflow: producing professional invoices without manually creating each document from scratch.

### Focus

- Invoice creation
- Structured business data
- Document generation
- Repeatable administrative workflows
- TypeScript application development

### Why I built it

Small businesses often spend time repeating tasks that can be represented as structured data and automated.

Invoice generation is a good example: customer details, products/services, quantities, prices, totals and invoice metadata can be captured once and transformed into a consistent document.

### Engineering perspective

```text
Business workflow
       ↓
Structured data
       ↓
Application logic
       ↓
Generated business document
```
